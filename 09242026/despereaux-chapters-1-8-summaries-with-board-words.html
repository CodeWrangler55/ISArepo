Literature Analysis Summary Packet

The Tale of Despereaux, Chapters 1-8 — teacher summaries, student-reader summaries, and board words.

Volunteer reader routine: Assign one student to each chapter. The student writes the chapter's board word, reads the summary aloud, and then the class answers one question. The board words can be reused in the word search.

The Tale of Despereaux — Chapters 1-8

Chapter 1

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux is born unusually small with huge ears and open eyes.

Student reader — board word: DIFFERENT
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux Tilling is born in a castle, but he is not like the other mice. He is tiny, has very large ears, and opens his eyes right away. His family expects him to be weak, but the narrator tells us his story will matter.

QuestionListen For
What makes Despereaux unusual when he is born?He is extremely small, has huge ears, and opens his eyes right away.
How does his family react to him?They worry about him and see him as disappointing or strange.
What character trait might Despereaux already show?Unusual, fragile, different, or special.

Chapter 2

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux lives, but he does not behave the way mice are expected to behave.

Student reader — board word: SMALL
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux survives, but he does not act like a normal mouse. He does not scurry properly or care much about crumbs. His differences make his family worry, but they also make him interesting.

QuestionListen For
How is Despereaux different from other mice?He does not act like a normal mouse: he is not focused on crumbs or scurrying.
Why does his family worry about him?They think his differences may be dangerous or shameful for a mouse.
Is being different always bad? Why or why not?No. Despereaux's differences may make him brave, curious, and important.

Chapter 3

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux follows music instead of acting like a fearful mouse.

Student reader — board word: MUSIC
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux hears beautiful music and follows it instead of hiding. Most mice would run away from humans, but Despereaux is drawn toward beauty and sound. This shows he is curious and brave in an unusual way.

QuestionListen For
What does Despereaux hear?He hears beautiful music.
Why is following the music risky?It leads him closer to humans, which mice are supposed to avoid.
What trait does this choice show?Curiosity and bravery.

Chapter 4

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux becomes interested in Princess Pea and the world of light and music.

Student reader — board word: PRINCESS
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux sees Princess Pea and King Phillip. He is fascinated by the princess and the music around her. Instead of hiding, he moves closer to the human world.

QuestionListen For
Who does Despereaux see?He sees Princess Pea and King Phillip.
How does he react to Princess Pea?He is fascinated by her and drawn toward her.
Why is this unusual for a mouse?Mice are supposed to hide from humans, not admire or approach them.

Chapter 5

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux reads a story instead of nibbling the book.

Student reader — board word: STORY
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux discovers a book and does not eat the paper like a normal mouse. Instead, he reads the story. He loves the ideas of knights, princesses, bravery, and happy endings.

QuestionListen For
What does Despereaux do with the book?He reads the story instead of eating the paper.
Why is this strange for a mouse?Mice usually nibble paper; Despereaux cares about the story.
How might stories affect Despereaux's choices?Stories teach him ideas about bravery, knights, princesses, and honor.

Chapter 6

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux is expected to follow mouse rules, but he does not fit them well.

Student reader — board word: RULES
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux's family tries to make him behave like other mice. They want him to learn mouse rules, such as searching for crumbs and staying away from humans. Despereaux struggles because his heart is drawn to different things.

QuestionListen For
What do the other mice expect Despereaux to do?They expect him to follow mouse rules, search for food, and stay hidden.
Why are rules important to the mouse community?Rules help mice stay safe from humans and danger.
What trait makes Despereaux struggle with these rules?His curiosity, bravery, and love of beauty make him break rules.

Chapter 7

Teacher — 1 sentence

Despereaux speaks to the princess instead of hiding from her.

Student reader — board word: BRAVE
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux goes near Princess Pea and speaks to her. This breaks a major mouse rule because mice are supposed to hide from humans. His action shows courage, but it also puts him in danger.

QuestionListen For
What brave thing does Despereaux do?He speaks to Princess Pea instead of hiding from her.
Why is this dangerous?Talking to a human breaks mouse law and could put him in danger.
How is Despereaux different from other mice in this chapter?He acts with wonder and courage instead of fear.

Chapter 8

Teacher — 1 sentence

The Mouse Council learns that Despereaux broke mouse rules.

Student reader — board word: COUNCIL
Write this word on the board before or after reading the chapter summary.

3rd-grade read-aloud

Despereaux's behavior is reported to the Mouse Council. The mice see his actions as dangerous because he has spoken to a human. The chapter shows that Despereaux's differences may have serious consequences.

QuestionListen For
Why is the Mouse Council upset?They hear that Despereaux has spoken to a human.
What rule did Despereaux break?He broke the rule that mice must not reveal themselves or speak to humans.
Do you think Despereaux is wrong or just different?Accept either answer if students support it with evidence from his actions.
