Literature Analysis Reading Lists

Working lists for ISA Literature Analysis tutoring. Use the school’s official list if it differs.

Class labels: MySchoolWorx may show ranges, but in class use 4th Grade and 8th Grade.

4th Grade

  1. Charlotte's Web by E. B. White
  2. The Tale of Despereaux by Kate DiCamillo
  3. Island of the Blue Dolphins by Scott O'Dell
  4. The Miraculous Journey of Edward Tulane by Kate DiCamillo
  5. The Trumpet of the Swan by E. B. White
  6. Pippi Longstocking by Astrid Lindgren
  7. Short stories and poetry

8th Grade

  1. Sarah, Plain and Tall by Patricia MacLachlan
  2. Holes by Louis Sachar
  3. The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe by C. S. Lewis
  4. A Christmas Carol by Charles Dickens
  5. A Wrinkle in Time by Madeleine L'Engle
  6. Code Name Kingfisher by Liz Kessler
  7. The Indian in the Cupboard by Lynne Reid Banks
